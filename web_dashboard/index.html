Methodology & Terminology

⭐ Composite Score A normalized 0-100 ranking combining valuation, analyst sentiment, and risk metrics. A score closer to 100 indicates a highly favorable quantitative profile relative to the current market.
Expected Upside The percentage difference between the current stock price and the average analyst price target. Indicates the theoretical growth potential based on institutional consensus.
Momentum Measures consecutive period-over-period positive changes in the underlying Total Value Score. A higher number indicates sustained sequential improvement over recent scraper runs.
